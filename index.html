Our Story

Built on a foundation
of trust and quality

Founded in 1962 by Mr. Sudhir Gupta, Shakti Group began as a hosiery manufacturer and has grown into a diversified conglomerate under the leadership of his sons, Sachin Gupta and Sumit Gupta.

Today we operate across four high-growth sectors: Textiles, Fenestration, Clean Energy, and Digital Connectivity — serving clients across India and beyond.
